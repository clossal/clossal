## Featured work

### [Evidence-Grounded Research Engine](https://github.com/clossal/evidence-grounded-research-engine)

A local research workspace for permitted PDFs: page-level evidence, cross-source
analysis, citation validation, insufficient-evidence handling, and reviewable
research memory. The public repository is source-only and does not redistribute
publisher documents, model weights, indexes, or private research records.

### [Customer Support Automation & Human Review](https://github.com/clossal/customer-support-automation-human-review)

A runnable local workflow that classifies sample support records, retrieves FAQ
sources, prepares grounded drafts, requires human review, and records decisions
in SQLite. It intentionally does not send email or touch customer data.

### [Solana ML Dashboard](https://github.com/clossal/solana-ml-dashboard)

A Streamlit demonstration using synthetic Solana-style data for feature
engineering, a lightweight classification model, trend clustering, and a simple
backtest. It is a portfolio prototype—not financial advice or a live signal.

### [Telegram Strategy Simulator](https://github.com/clossal/Telegram-Strategy-Simulator)

An interactive Streamlit simulator for exploring strategy thresholds, risk
controls, model signals, and backtest results on synthetic market-style data.
